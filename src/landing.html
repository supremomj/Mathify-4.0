Why Mathify?

Everything students need to love math

Built for Filipino classrooms and aligned with the DepEd MATATAG curriculum, Mathify makes math genuinely fun.

Interactive Games

Engaging game-based learning that turns every math problem into a mini-adventure. Students stay motivated with points, streaks, and unlockable rewards.

AI-Powered

Adaptive lessons that adjust to each student's pace. Get personalized recommendations and instant, encouraging feedback after every answer.

Curriculum-Aligned

Fully aligned with the DepEd MATATAG curriculum for Grades 1–6. Students learn exactly what they need for their grade level — nothing more, nothing less.

Track Progress

Detailed progress dashboards for students. Earn coins, collect badges, and watch your level grow as you master new math concepts.
